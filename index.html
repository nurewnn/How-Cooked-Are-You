How It Works

A Mamdani fuzzy inference system that evaluates your semester survival

What is this?

This simulator uses fuzzy logic to calculate your "survival score" based on four key inputs: assignment load, sleep, wallet, and stress. Instead of binary true/false, fuzzy logic handles the grey areas — "kind of stressed", "somewhat broke", "barely sleeping" — and turns all that into one crisp score.

How does it work?

1You talk to the diary.
Share what your semester was like — the good, the bad, the maggi.
2Variables are extracted.
Your words are analyzed to estimate assignment load, sleep, wallet, and stress.
381 fuzzy rules fire.
Every combination of the four variables is checked. Matching rules fire with different strengths.
4A crisp score is calculated.
All fired rules are combined via Center of Gravity — giving you one survival score (0-100).

What is fuzzy logic?

Unlike traditional logic where things are true (1) or false (0), fuzzy logic lets things be partially true. So instead of "you're either stressed or not", fuzzy logic says you might be 30% Chilling, 70% Concerned, and 0% Screaming. The Mamdani method processes these partial truths through rules and produces a crisp, actionable output.
